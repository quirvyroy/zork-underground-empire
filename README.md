## ❓ Need Help? Troubleshooting

Even the smoothest games sometimes hiccup. Here are quick fixes for common issues.

- **Game won't load:** First, check your internet connection. Then, try refreshing the page. If it still doesn't load, close your browser completely and reopen it. That often clears up problems.
- **Images look stretched or blurry:** That's normal. The game adjusts its graphics to fit your screen. For the best experience, try making your browser window larger or going fullscreen (usually the **F11** key on Windows).
- **No sound:** Check that your computer's volume isn't muted and that the browser's tab isn't silent (right-click the tab and select "Unmute Site").
- **Game feels slow:** This usually means your computer is working hard. Try closing other browser tabs or programs. Also, make sure to update your browser to the latest version.
- **Controls feel weird:** Read the on-screen instructions again. Some players prefer mouse-only, while others like keyboard. The game supports both, so try what feels right for you.
